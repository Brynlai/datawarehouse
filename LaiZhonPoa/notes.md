### 3.1.1 Annual Revenue Performance and Growth

*   **Findings:**
    *   16-year history: growth phases, a minor recession (2013-15).
    *   2020 Pandemic: major drop (-28.79%).
    *   2021-22 Rebound: strong recovery, all-time high revenue ($7.75M in 2022).
    *   Post-2022: slight decline, signs of market normalization.
    *   2025 data: incomplete, shows a misleading drop.
*   **Significance:**
    *   Demonstrates business resilience to major shocks.
    *   Successfully captured "pent-up demand" post-pandemic.
    *   Recent decline is a concern, may signal post-shock overspending correction.
*   **Causes:**
    *   **2020 Drop:** COVID-19 travel shutdowns.
    *   **2021-22 Growth:** Lifting of restrictions, high travel urge.
    *   **2023 Decline:** Inflation, normalization of travel trends, increased competition (e.g., Airbnb).
*   **Actions:**
    *   Reduce operational costs for future resilience.
    *   Adapt to competition (e.g., list rooms on Airbnb).
    *   Launch targeted campaigns (family/deluxe rooms for higher pricing power).
    *   Forecast conservatively for 2026 (-3% to -5%) to manage budgets.

### 3.1.2 Quarterly Performance Analysis

*   **Findings:**
    *   Consistent, predictable seasonal cycle.
    *   Q4 is the peak revenue season (holidays).
    *   Q1 is the weakest season (post-holiday slump).
    *   Recent years show an alarming decline in Q4 performance (-4.7% in 2023).
*   **Significance:**
    *   Annual revenue is highly dependent on Q4 performance.
    *   A weak Q4 cripples the entire year's results.
    *   The negative Q4 trend threatens the core financial model.
    *   Relying solely on the holiday season is now a risky strategy.
*   **Causes:**
    *   **Q4 Peak:** End-of-year commercial and public holidays.
    *   **Q1 Slump:** Post-holiday "spending hangover."
    *   **Recent Q4 Decline:** Increased competition and economic pressures (inflation).
*   **Actions:**
    *   Defend Q4: launch marketing campaigns earlier (Sept 1st).
    *   Mitigate Q1 Slump: target corporate clients, offer all-inclusive deals for events.
    *   Use promotions to fill rooms during off-peak times.
    *   Align operational budgets with seasonal performance expectations.

### 3.1.3 Hotel Upselling Performance

*   **Findings:**
    *   Metric: "Upselling Ratio" measures extra spending vs. room rate.
    *   Huge performance gap between hotels (e.g., North Christopher at 10.9% vs. Lake Aaron at 6%).
    *   High room rates do not guarantee high upselling ratios.
*   **Significance:**
    *   Massive untapped profit potential exists in underperforming hotels.
    *   Upselling improves profitability without raising room rates (higher margins).
    *   Crucial for revenue growth in a challenging market.
*   **Causes:**
    *   **Success:** Tailored local offerings, effective staff sales training, compelling packages.
    *   **Underperformance:** Ineffective sales processes, lack of targeted offerings.
*   **Actions:**
    *   Create a "Best Practices" playbook from top performers.
    *   Pilot the playbook at the 3 lowest-performing hotels.
    *   Implement intensive, onsite staff training.
    *   Set measurable goal: Increase Upselling Ratio by 3 percentage points.
    *   Tie staff bonuses to achieving upselling targets.
